About Me

I'm Princess Kalu, a social media manager who loves building content strategies that make a brand's online presence impossible to scroll past. Over the past year, I've worked across hospitality, wellness, manufacturing, tech, and home fragrance, positioning each account with a voice that fits its audience, then backing that strategy with paid growth so the right people actually find it.

The results speak for themselves. I grew a hospitality client's reach by 91% during a full brand relaunch, and drove an 83% month over month increase in engagement for a home fragrance client's anniversary campaign. My content doesn't just speak to the people already following an account, on most brands I manage, 80 to 95 percent of the accounts reached are non-followers. That tells me the strategy is working, the content is being discovered, and the audience is growing.

I'm a skilled Meta Ads manager. I run campaigns start to finish, targeting, budget, creative testing, optimization. On one brand's anniversary campaign I managed, the paid strategy drove an 83% jump in engagement. I read the results closely enough to adjust a campaign before it underperforms, not after.

At the core of it, I care about social media management that ties back to something real. More reach, more engagement, and more of the right people finding a brand for the first time.
