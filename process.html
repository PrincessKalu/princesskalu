Process

How a Project Comes Together

No two brands need the same thing, but the path to get there follows the same shape.

01

Discovery & Audit

A close look at the brand's current accounts, audience, and competitors — what's working, what's not, and where the actual opportunity is.

02

Strategy & Content Pillars

A content strategy built around clear pillars and a real posting cadence, tied to the brand's voice and business goals — not just trends.

03

Production & Scheduling

Content calendars, captions, and creative get built and scheduled in advance, so the account stays consistent even on busy weeks.

04

Paid Amplification

Where it makes sense, Meta Ads campaigns are layered on top — targeted, tested, and optimized against real budget and ROI goals.

05

Reporting & Iteration

Performance gets reviewed against the numbers that matter — reach, engagement, non-follower discovery — and the strategy adjusts from there.
